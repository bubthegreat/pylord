# LORD IGMs to port

683 In-Game Module archives mirrored from
https://mirrors.archeobits.com/bbs/archives.thebbs.org/ra98c.htm (fetched 2026-07-24).

These are the original DOS-era LORD IGM distributions. The plan is to work through
them over time and port the ones worth keeping to pylord.

## Takedown requests

These archives are preserved here as raw material for porting, in the same
spirit as the BBS archive mirrors they came from. If you are the author or
rights holder of any of these files and would like one removed — or have any
concern about an IGM being ported — email **bubthegreat@gmail.com** and it
will be taken down, no questions asked.

## Removed: adult-content IGMs

14 archives whose documentation marks them as adult content were deliberately
excluded (adultmon, ba506, carnal15, caverhp2, eros70, hongv104, inns100,
ldwhr13, para199, para1991, rue15, turttb10, turttb12, whore20). Bawdy-but-mild
IGMs on par with base LORD (Violet, the Inn) were kept.

## Known oddities

- `tense100.zip` is byte-identical to the mirror's copy but has a damaged or
  nonstandard central directory; period tools may still extract it.
- 8 files listed on the index page 404 on the mirror and could not be fetched:
  $word11.zip, galigms.zip, iw_01.zip, jewel12.zip, liddux16.zip,
  odink200.zip, olman20.zip, ship009.zip

Shelves generated by a one-time scan (2026-07-25); classifications are best-effort and corrected at port time.
